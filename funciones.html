Every feature in detail

A tour of every tab in the teacher dashboard, the phone dashboard, assignment and question types, banks, the voice conversation generator, reports and the principal’s dashboard. Screenshots use demo data.

01 / Teacher dashboard on the computerTabs and tools

The top bar holds the main tabs and, on the right, the school year picker, messages 💬, notifications 🔔, the theme switch and My account.

👥 Classes

Your classes for the current school year, with their students and attendance.

  • Create classes with a name, color, class language and schedule, even with several sessions on the same day.
  • Invitation code so students can join on their own, or enroll them from Accounts.
  • Students with color indicators based on their attendance and submissions; you set the percentage for each color.
  • View details for each student: attendance session by session, and grades.
  • Private note on each student that only you can see, and direct message.
  • Attendance: sessions by QR or roll call, a grace period in minutes, one device per student, and reopening sessions.
  • Class settings: logo for PDFs, graphing tool and chat for students.
  • Reorder classes by dragging them.
Example

You can scan each student’s QR code manually. The class traffic light already shows who is piling up absences.

📚 Content

Each class’s units and all their activities.

  • Units with their own color and order.
  • ＋ New assignment with the 11 available types.
  • ⋮ menu on each activity: edit, view submissions, duplicate, hide, schedule when it becomes visible, assign it to only some students, generate a paper exam and export to Google Forms.
  • Full-screen form editor with sections, a preview that simulates what students will see, and multi-select to move, copy or save questions to a bank.
  • Student banks per unit: definitions and review questions you can show them.
  • Import and export activities between classes.
Example

You prepare a quiz once and duplicate it to your three classes of the same subject. In one of them you schedule it to appear Monday at 8:00 and assign it only to the students who need to make up the unit.

🗂 Banks

Your reusable material, organized by subject, unit and folders.

  • Five bank types: questions, dictionaries, materials, activities and exams.
  • Explorer with tabs by type, a folder tree, search, and nested folders you can rename.
  • Insert from the bank into any form, even a whole scenario with all its questions.
  • Form from a bank: each student gets a different selection of questions.
Example

Every semester you save your best questions in the “Biology › Unit 1” bank. The next semester you build the exam by picking 20 at random out of 60, and each student gets a different combination.

📊 Grading

Grades calculated automatically, unit by unit.

  • Weights per unit: attendance, assignments, projects, exam and your own criteria.
  • Custom criteria graded manually, with a checklist or with a rubric.
  • Weight between units, equal or custom, for the final grade.
  • Extra credit, game bonuses, and trading game points for grade points.
  • Summary table for the unit with each criterion’s calculation per student.
  • PDF and Excel reports.
Example

For Unit 2 you decide attendance is worth 10%, homework 40%, the project 20% and the exam 30%. When you close the unit, you download the PDF report to sign and the spreadsheet for your records.

🎮 Games

Thirteen multiplayer games for review in the classroom.

  • EduQuiz, EduCard, EduFlip, EduDuel (spaceship duel), EduFix (spot the mistake), EduRace (question race), EduSort (lightning sorting), EduLine (timeline), EduStop (categories game), EduRain (word rain), EduCatch (catch the answers), EduBlast (verb balloons) and EduWord (hidden word).
  • EduQuiz: live questions with points for accuracy, speed and streaks; music, countdown and results play only on the teacher’s computer; podium and statistics at the end. Questions from your banks and dictionaries.
  • EduCard: cards with words or pictures; the winning pattern (line, corners, L, T, full board or a drawn shape) is chosen in the waiting room; it ends with the first winner; students have to notice on their own that they’ve won. List editor with pictures.
  • EduFlip: real-time memory, every student with their own board and everyone at once; configurable time with extra time, pause, live leaderboard, and awards without exposing mistakes.
  • Computer and phone at the same time: start the game on either one; the other gets a "Go to game controls" prompt and it can be controlled from both. Sounds play on the classroom computer.
  • Room with a lobby: students join while you choose the questions.
  • Only the chosen class sees the game session.
  • Scores per student that can count toward the unit.
Example

Ten minutes before the end of class, you open EduQuiz with the unit bank’s questions, students join from their phones and review by competing.

🎬 Present

Your presentations with interactive content for each slide.

  • Editor per slide: text, poll, true or false, multiple choice, image, audio, video or link.
  • Live control from the computer or your phone: advance, lock or pin content, free navigation and random student.
  • Teacher mode: only you use the controls, without taking students into the presentation.
  • Screenshot to send students what you’re showing.
  • Download of the presentation for the audience, during or at the end.
  • Resume on the slide where you left off with that class.
Example

On slide 3 of your lesson about the cell, each student gets the question “Which organelle produces energy?” on their phone. You instantly see how many got it right before you explain.

📦 Lessons

Complete lessons built as a sequence of steps.

  • Attendance, presentation, announcement, bank activity and timer steps.
  • Suggested minutes per step: the system suggests moving on, but you decide.
  • Start and control them from the computer or your phone.
Example

Your 50-minute lesson: 3 for roll call, 20 for the presentation, 2 for a quick question and 15 for the quiz. You repeat it exactly the same with every class in a single click.

👤 Accounts

Your students’ accounts.

  • Create students with last and first names, and enroll them in your classes.
  • Filter by class and move students from one class to another.
  • Activate or deactivate accounts, reset passwords, and unlock anyone who entered the wrong password too many times.
  • The principal creates the teacher accounts; each teacher sees only their own students.

⚙️ Settings

Platform settings. If the school has a principal’s account, backups, updates, security and guest mode are managed from their dashboard; teachers keep the language and their problem reports.

  • Language for your dashboard: Spanish or English.
  • Updates: check for, download and install new versions.
  • Automatic backup to the folder or USB drive you choose, as often as you like and with whatever you decide to include.
  • Restore a backup by picking it with Browse, merging it with your data or replacing it.
  • Security: lockout after failed password attempts.
  • Guest mode and a problem log for support.

⚡ Quick tools

A menu in the top bar with the tools you use during class, without switching tabs.

  • 🎲 Random student and 👥 teams.
  • ⏱ Full-screen timer that students see too.
  • 🎤 Noise traffic light, 🔗 participation chain and 💀 survival.
  • 📢 Live announcement and a full-screen 📲 QR code for students to sign in.
  • 📐 Graphing tool and 🎙 voice conversations.
Example

You notice the class getting distracted: you open the noise traffic light, which turns red when the volume rises, and then use the spinner to pick who answers next.

🧭 School years, messages, notifications and My account

  • School years: create a new one, copy your classes over and switch years; each teacher has their own.
  • Message center with each student’s history.
  • Notifications: choose which of the nine alert types you want to receive, such as late submissions or finished exams.
  • My account: photo, password, your weekly schedule and the order of the phone bar.

06 / Voice conversationsWith your ElevenLabs account

Turn a written script into a conversation with a different voice for each character, and download it as an MP3 to use in forms, interactive audio or presentations. Open it from Quick tools → Voice conversations.

Connect your ElevenLabs account

  1. Sign in at elevenlabs.io with your account.
  2. Go to Developers → API Keys and create a key. You can limit it to the Text to Speech and Voices permissions.
  3. Copy the key, paste it into EduOffline and press Connect.

The key is stored only on your EduOffline server; it’s never shown in full or sent to students. Each teacher connects their own account, and usage consumes your ElevenLabs plan’s credits. Requires internet.

Create the conversation

  1. Write the script, one line per turn: David: Hi Maria, how was your weekend?
  2. EduOffline detects the speakers and assigns voices based on their names; you can change them.
  3. Listen to the preview and download the MP3.

If you generate an audio that already exists, it’s reused without spending credits again.

Example

For an English lesson you write a dialogue between two students arriving at school. In a minute you have the audio with two different voices and put it into an interactive audio with comprehension questions.

09 / Principal’s dashboardFor the school principal

The principal signs in with their own account, separate from any teacher’s, and lands on a separate dashboard for running the school. They oversee without stepping into each teacher’s work: they don’t see answers, submissions, messages or private notes, and everything they do is logged. The principal’s account is created when EduOffline is installed.

  • Home: students, teachers, classes, active connections, storage used, server and database status, latest backup and recent activity.
  • Teachers: create accounts, deactivate them (their session ends immediately and their data is kept), reset passwords and unlock. Clicking a teacher shows their classes, subjects and weekly schedule.
  • Students: everyone in the school, with search, filters by class and status (active, deactivated, locked, never signed in) and sorting alphabetically, by class or by last sign-in.
  • Classes: every class with its teacher. Transfer a class to another teacher with its students, units, assignments, submissions, grades and attendance; both teachers are notified.
  • Read-only grades for any class. Each view is logged, and teachers know the principal can see them.
  • School settings: backups and restore, updates, sign-in security, guest mode, and sending statistics and reports.
  • Audit log: teacher and principal sign-ins, failed attempts, account changes, transfers, grade views and settings changes.
Example

A teacher moves to another shift mid-semester. The principal transfers her classes to the replacement teacher: he finds them on his dashboard with all the grades and attendance, without re-entering anything.
