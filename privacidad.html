Privacy notice

EduOffline is a school program that runs on each school’s network, without needing internet. This notice explains what data this website handles and what data the program handles when a school or a teacher installs it.

01 / This websiteWhat happens when you visit
  • It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking tools, and it has no forms: it doesn’t collect your name, your email or any other data.
  • It shares nothing with third parties. Fonts, images and the sample 3D model are served from the site itself.
  • Light or dark theme: if you change it, your choice is saved only in your browser (local storage) so it’s remembered on your next visit. It isn’t sent anywhere, and you can delete it by clearing the site’s data in your browser.
  • Hosting: like any website, the service that hosts this site may log technical data about each visit (IP address, date and page visited) for its operation and security, under its own privacy notice.
  • Downloads: the installer is downloaded from GitHub, which has its own privacy notice.
02 / The EduOffline programData of schools, teachers and students

Data stays at the school

EduOffline is installed on a school or teacher computer. Accounts, submissions, grades, attendance and messages are stored on that computer and are not uploaded to the internet. The EduOffline team has no access to them.

The party responsible for that data is whoever administers each installation: the school, through its principal, or the teacher who uses EduOffline on their own. Each installation shows its own privacy notice inside the program, with the name and contact of the person responsible, and asks users to accept it when creating an account and whenever it changes. Underage students confirm that their parent or guardian is aware of it.

What reaches the EduOffline team, without names

To improve the program, when the school’s computer has internet it may send the EduOffline project:

  • Anonymous usage statistics: daily counts per school (which features are used, on what type of device, how long pages take to load and how many errors there are). They contain no personal data.
  • Satisfaction survey: it’s voluntary. If someone answers it, their answers are sent with a random code, their class, the device and browser type and a school code, with no name or username. The answers are also used, in aggregate, in a master’s degree research project on EduOffline’s usability.
  • Problem reports: what the user writes when reporting a problem and the technical context, with student and teacher names replaced.

This information is received in Google forms and spreadsheets and is used only to improve EduOffline and for the research mentioned above. It is not sold or shared with other companies. Whoever administers each installation can turn off each kind of sending in the program’s settings; when turned off, anything not yet sent is deleted.

03 / Your rights and contactAccess, correction, deletion and objection
  • Your school’s data (accounts, grades, attendance…): ask to see, correct or delete it from the person responsible named in your installation’s privacy notice, usually your school’s principal or your teacher.
  • Data sent to the EduOffline project: since it carries no names, we can’t link it to a person. If you answered the survey and want to withdraw your answers, share your participant code; for any other request, use "Report a problem" inside EduOffline or ask your teacher or your school’s principal.
  • Changes to this notice: if it changes, we’ll update this page and its date.
